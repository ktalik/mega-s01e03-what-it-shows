  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint: tenants on booking-v3 print MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. Only reports sent through that form. The parser fix does not touch it: the form has to print the tenant’s order.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not a day/month swap. The same meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before its booking; June bookings are a day off. This looks like a date-only value passed through a time zone. The desk had already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), and CauseTable footnotes them under parser instead of counting them apart.

Why the merge hid them

  • Intake’s hint tag comes from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant’s form. It is wrong in both directions, so it cannot be used to count.
  • The ticket export repeats reports across overlapping pages. Every count here is by unique id.
